# Native scaled history outcome policy v1

## Question

Can the complete-public-history structured model learn a seat-stable policy
direction from terminal outcomes when given the full 2,048-pair corpus?

This is a rapid development screen. Win, draw, or loss at the natural terminal
is the only reward. A positive offline result is not strength evidence.

## Fixed screen

- Cache: exact complete-history cache SHA-256
  `721aeeb8389464676edf1190b4e90d74ced286104cc0fb30deb46d36ffbc8090`.
- Data: 2,048 seat-swapped pairs and 4,096 natural games generated by the exact
  retained parent against XMage CP7.
- Split: four whole-pair folds by `pair_index mod 4`.
- Representation: width 48, last 16 complete public physical decisions,
  structured state, objects, relations, actions, references, and attention.
- Advantage: terminal reward minus frozen parent value, centered and scaled
  within candidate seat using equal episode mass.
- Objective: physical-decision joint-ratio PPO, clip 0.10, five epochs, batch
  size 64, AdamW learning rate `3e-4`, weight decay `1e-4`, gradient cap 5.
- Initialization: zero policy residual, exactly preserving the parent policy.
- Calibration: the final policy head may only be scaled down to fit mean total
  variation 0.03. It is never amplified.

A 128-pair, one-epoch throughput run may select execution topology. It cannot
change the scientific settings or count as evidence.

## Gate

Advance only if aggregate and both-seat held-out surrogates are positive, at
least three folds are positive, mean and p90 total variation are at most 0.03
and 0.10, maximum physical-decision joint log ratio is at most 0.50, object
permutation delta is at most `1e-5`, and at least 20 percent of sampled
reference-bearing decisions respond to reference removal.

A pass authorizes one full-data fit and a fresh rapid matched live gate. Fit
all 2,048 pairs once with the fixed configuration and calibrate on a fixed
8,192-decision sample. The live gate uses base seed `1260001`, the lowest 16
pair indices where both candidate and parent complete, and outcome-blind
mapper exclusions. Pass only if `G >= L + 3`, candidate-minus-parent win count
is at least `-2` separately at P0 and P1, and all transport checks pass. A
failure retires this exact scaled terminal-PPO package without tuning it on the
revealed folds.

## Completed development result

All four folds completed concurrently in 51.5 minutes with no stderr. Overall
held-out surrogate was `+0.000131120`; candidate P0 was `+0.000158234` and P1
was `+0.000104006`. All four folds were positive. Mean total variation was
`0.00423422`, p90 total variation was `0.0149421`, and maximum physical-
decision joint log ratio was `0.393274`. All representation checks passed.

The aggregate passed every gate and authorizes the fixed full-data fit and
fresh live gate above. Aggregate SHA-256 is
`9e3f78918f93c78fc999acb8e5524709f89d3244804d64cedbf4d030cc1b9c9c`.
