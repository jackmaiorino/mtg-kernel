# Bounded terminal-teaching retention comparison

**Question.** Does an explicit parent-policy penalty reduce unrelated policy movement while preserving tactical learning? This is a local feasibility experiment leading, at most, to a later whole-match test. It cannot establish human/league strength or promote a model. **Fixed inputs and arms.** Start every arm from original g115 with its full Adam state at age 32,400. Use the original 32 training positions, corrected semantic labels `terminal-semantic-teacher-labels-001/labels.json` (SHA `e498b35653cc012f6dc2de84719ceb0225ecd0290adde466124beec9e4456233`), and the 256-group/303-row retention data `terminal-retention-data-002/train.json` (SHA `701f816a4f51ce66bc356e69112693151a856a354c429d4c0a9efb8cc8fa5414`). Three arms: correct labels/beta 0; correct labels/beta 0.5; semantic control labels/beta 0.5. Beta 0.5 carries forward the diagnostic setting, not a validation-selected optimum. Fixed learning rate 0.0001, 32 full-batch updates, no reward/value-loss substitution, no new entropy term, no checkpoint selection or intermediate validation. Use the existing separately normalized teacher CE and parent KL; retain all original optimizer moments. Correct and control targets are imitation labels, not rewritten gameplay returns.

**Evaluation.** Only original g115 and the three final endpoints are scored. Evaluate training fit descriptively, the 48 newly constructed tactical cases (32 unique-win and 16 no-win controls), and the reserved 100 retention games. Pin the completed tactical data receipt before any candidate launch. The consumed old 24-position evaluation is excluded. No CP7 outcomes enter selection. Report every tactical family/seat; no-win cases supply no optimal-action accuracy label. Retention metrics use production Hamilton probabilities on saved visible inputs. Compute TV versus g115 for each multi-action row, mean rows within physical decisions, mean decisions within each game, then equal-weight the 100 games. Also report top-action changes, per-deck/seat/category effects and value movement. Parent agreement is not proof of correctness.

**Frozen advancement gates.** All required jobs/cases/checkpoints must complete and replay correctly. Both correct-label arms must fit at least 15/16 training targets in each tactical family; the semantic control must fit at least 28/32 of its own training targets, otherwise the control comparison is inconclusive. On fresh tactical cases, the retained arm must select a certified winner on at least 14/16 face and 14/16 creature cases, lose no more than two correct choices per family versus the unretained arm, exceed original g115 by at least two correct choices overall and exceed the semantic-control endpoint by at least four. These are correlated fixture counts, not binomial population confidence claims. For policy retention, the retained arm's equal-game mean TV must be at most 80% of the unretained arm's mean TV, and the upper bound of a paired 95% percentile bootstrap interval for retained-minus-unretained TV must be below zero. Resample the ten archive update blocks, preserving all ten paired games per block; 20,000 resamples, NumPy PCG64 seed 2026092101. Zero unretained mean TV is an uninformative retention comparison and does not pass. Passing every gate authorizes consideration of a separately designed matched whole-match experiment, not adoption. Any failure remains a failure; no changed weight, endpoint or gate is selected from this panel.
