## Distinct successor protocol

`python/tools/current_actor_probe_v1.py prepare` writes a fresh versioned
manifest without launching games. It retains all80/86 deck inventory rows and
the exact historical planned60 cases, using the recovered43/3 order rather
than refiltering the schedule against today's broader registry. Requirements
come from the original log's reported deck pairs and decision kinds. Every
supplied input receives a hash. Original40 Limited and historical widened60
are separate named cohorts. Eternal retains its published60/15 lists.

The prospective primary120-game panel is original40 Limited60 plus Eternal60.
The historical60 Limited manifest is an optional additional comparison, never
a replacement for original40 acceptance. Its copy mixture changed during the
old RT1 widening. Historic60 mainboards use the old sorted-ID ordering before
environment-v2 shuffling; original40 keeps the supplied deck row order.

The new selector is seeded rejection-sampled uniform over the genuine legal
menu. Every current-tree deterministic-fixture logit is still evaluated and
checked for arity and finiteness; logits do not control this diagnostic
selector. The seed is the historical environment seed XOR `0x454a11`.
There is no learning, checkpoint selection or strength comparison. New Limited
runtime includes Foundations combat and London, whereas the old RT1 template
used resolution-boundary rules without those openings. Identical labels and
seeds do not establish identical initial states or policy trajectories.

The ignored native entry is
`current_actor_probe_v1::reported_pool_digest_free_terminal_probe`. It uses
sealed capture and action application, including separate binding and native
step error paths. Configuration, owning-session construction, encoding,
scoring, action-audit, native engine and cap/incomplete failures are distinct.
Every game receives a record even when it fails; nothing is silently skipped.
The report retains exact safe decision headers, reached action kinds, both
flag values, natural terminal classification, and an actor-input/action/logit
trace hash. Native world state and hidden continuation frames are not dumped.

The old probe's percentage measured pairs distinguished only by digest tails.
This successor has no digest tails. Its replacement diagnostic counts distinct
typed actions that have equal complete action trees plus six flags, and also
counts equal signatures after neutralizing only the root candidate ordinal.
The latter prevents a vacuous pass caused solely by candidate position. Nested
reference ordinals and every other operand remain. This is audit-only and does
not change scorer inputs. A collision is unresolved evidence, not automatically
proof of a particular missing rule. The new rate is not the old digest-only rate.

Panel acceptance requires all60 planned games to terminate naturally, zero
unresolved ordinal-neutral action collisions, and every reported pair/kind to
be reached. A decision cap, halted terminal, failed creation or eight-step
opening is never completion. The paid Doomsday fixture is a new explicit
range witness, not a reconstruction of the old unretained InvalidActionRange
decision. Existing source tests and terminal coverage remain separate gates.
