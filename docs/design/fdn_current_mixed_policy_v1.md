# Current mixed numeric/scorer v1: implementation and qualification

Original base: `4e20c0255f75064993df4a2ac40a57b070d7334b`. V52 base:
`a7ff87bf8bc0db655db07548d9f2920bb0f99caa`. The five original model commits
through `7663e98b4` are composed with attributed cherry-picks. The current source
has distinct owning numeric entries through Kaito V75: Fake, Ravenous, Elenda,
Prepared, NextFive, GraveyardReturn, Call, EndTurn, GenesisWave, Vivien,
Muldrotha, Tinybones, Liliana, Chandra and Kaito. The V50/V51/V52 design below
describes the original layers. Later entries retain the inherited typed facts
and add their own rules, original action bindings and ordinary printings.
Implementation and executed checks do not by themselves admit the whole pool,
new model checkpoints, search or training. Frozen encoders and measurements
retain their existing contracts.

Separate V50, V51 and V52 owning entries consume genuine typed observations,
original ordered ActionSemantic values and complete private family bindings.
Apply authenticates the original owner, generation and current binding, then
regenerates the numeric packet before the real ordered session step. Snapshots
retain owner identity; restore advances backend revision and generation. V52
uses the existing sealed Elenda owner and exact V51 numeric header helper after
its existing V52 mode guard. Capabilities and executable candidates stay private.

The schema-labelled numeric tree retains array order, field/category tokens and
exact signed/unsigned magnitudes. Tags, UTF8 bytes and four 16-bit integer limbs
are exactly representable in f32; topology ordinals use the same limbs. There
is no 255-target or seven-trigger cap. Physical references become deterministic
roster ordinals, incarnations become relationship ordinals and timestamps become
chronological ranks. Names, text, opaque handles, authority and provenance hashes
are omitted. Unknown reference shapes refuse. This is a typed-value visitor,
not JSON byte/hash features, and no raw EffectOp or private world enters tensors.

Existing typed family producers expand root references into their ordered meaning.
A family root alone does not establish complete printing coverage.
Exact baseline descriptors cover intrinsic land mana, vanilla/keyword creatures,
Llanowar mana, Bolt and Treasure. Think Twice retains normal Hand `{1}{U}` and
own Graveyard flashback `{2}{U}`, Draw1 order and flashback exile replacement.
Counterspell, Essence Scatter, Refute and Offer retain exact costs, target filters,
counter eligibility and physical/flashback/copy departure; Refute preserves
counter/draw/private-discard order. Offer samples the target spell controller
before counter and rewards it independently using the full Treasure descriptor.
Legacy zone-only guards remain distinct from Limited's protected-spell guards.
Descriptors grant no cast permission or executable payment plan.

V52 adds Elenda's typed ordered lifelink, opponent Instant-source protection and
both additive starting-life bonuses. Its ordinary cast root represents printed
`{2}{W}{B}`, sorcery timing, no targets and Stack-to-Battlefield entry under the
caster. Authenticated current facts retain effective form, controller, threshold
conditions and applied bonuses. Baseline selectors preserve the old 69 CardDef
checks and explicitly exclude starting-life bonuses, source-type protection and
Spinner's death descriptor. The Elenda root authenticates these separately.

Conqueror and Season now receive mandatory ordinary-printing supplements for
actually actor-authorized referenced cards, independently of the generic
covered-ID set. Exact 72-field selectors validate numeric printed mana costs,
card types/subtypes/colors/stats/ordered keywords and the ordinary Hand cast,
sorcery timing, no targets, entry under the caster and physical departure.
Existing trigger/replacement meanings and actual custody facts are preserved;
unsupported changed shapes refuse. No permission or executable is encoded.
Current normalization applies registered ordinary permanent and spell printing
supplements independently of the family covered-ID set. A family root alone
still cannot establish complete printing coverage. Full-pool opening-hand
coverage is now an explicit test over every frozen printing and both seats;
its newly authored case remains unexecuted. This checks actual owning
observation, numeric encoding and finite scoring of the issued candidates.
It does not replace paid branch, continuation or hidden-information checks.

The distinct network has explicit input20/hidden16 dimensions, ordered tree
recurrence and state/action/product readout. Its source/catalog feature identity
and parameter contract reject predecessor checkpoints. Forward returns exactly
one finite logit per original action or an error. Fixture weights demonstrate
arithmetic only, with no learning, playing-strength or generalization claim.

The original V52 implementation refused later families such as Drake, Spinner,
Quandary, Phoenix and Horde. Current successor entries implement those typed
paths; predecessor contracts retain their scoped refusals. Missing typed roots
or reference shapes must still refuse explicitly. The full-pool requirement
remains open until current native qualification proves every required ordinary
printing, paid branch and family interaction. There is no complete-pool model,
sampler/search, training or evaluation admission yet.

Authored tests retain both-seat paid Fake/Bolt death-return-Treasure, Amulet,
Think Twice normal/flashback, actual counterspell outcomes, complete binding and
foreign/stale/restore refusal, exact action-index parity, private-world invariance,
wide V51 values and genuine 260-target Divine with restore at255. Five V52 groups
add paid Elenda/Amulet coexistence, exact wide noncreature values, finite forward,
ordered candidate parity, forged packet refusal, repeated owning restore,
independent hidden worlds and paid own-Bolt targeting with full replay parity.
Four further groups assert exact Conqueror/Season printing semantics, genuine
paid casts through V50/V51 and inherited V52 owners with ordered-index/gameplay restore
parity, mandatory supplements despite catalog meanings and initial hidden-hand
invariance. These are part of the current qualification suite, not proof that
every later source change passes it.
