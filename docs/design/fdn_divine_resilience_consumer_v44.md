mixed public transport, sealed backend binding and fixed-seat owning Human

unrun on this source.

`Mage.Sets/src/mage/cards/d/DivineResilience.java`. The spell costs W and has

## Visibility and restore

Safe card handles are canonicalized with the selected and legal roster roles,
quote sources, finalized target ordinals and all prior-family roles. Raw arena,
definition, incarnation, journal and root identifiers never enter the Human
DTO. Current face-down targets use the existing masked sentinel. Departed
target visibility comes from the independently committed, validated original
battlefield departure snapshot. It is not reconstructed from a newer hidden
incarnation. The private original target contract remains in backend authority.
The actual shared stack producer has a separate Divine acceptance bit, false
for every earlier scope. V44 derives its public stack target rows directly
from those authenticated original visibility facts, as well as its supplement.
It does not mask a previously published frozen artifact after construction.
The full source catalog contains the existing departure-capture descriptor;
missing or duplicate original visibility evidence is refused.

The owning Human adapter binds requests and accepted retries to its allocation
owner and monotonic restore generation outside snapshots. Backend restore first
authenticates the original snapshot header/menu, rebases the actual owned
combat surface and both current/session revisions to checked max+1, then
publishes once. Retained pending or accepted requests cannot revive after
rollback. Same-generation accepted retry remains idempotent.

## Remaining limits

The new consumer handles the exact ordinary hand-cast Divine producer. Copied
variable spells and foreign variable producers are explicitly refused. A
copied recipient whose historical definition cannot be represented by the
existing common target projection also retains that refusal. The existing
physical arena and action-index capacity checks remain explicit; they do not
create a target-cardinality rule.

The earlier LimitedCommon JSONL namespace retains its Divine refusal. A caller
must explicitly use the new V44 backend/Human adapter; enabling this mode is
not a model or whole-pool acceptance claim. Frozen model tensors lack the new
wide cardinality/ordered-selection/quote representation. Search and sampler
admission need their own sufficient observation, action and authority
successors and actual qualification. V44 preserves all current family refusal
boundaries, including unsupported copied or hidden producer projections.
