# Visible life Ward before targeting

Source preparation for issue110, based on the typed V8 transport at a6b6a56f.
Sire remains unregistered. Native qualification of this change and all six
actual Sire fixture groups remain pending; no coverage or training result.

V8's pending and queued payments identify a fee after a target is announced.
The frozen effective-keyword fields expose generic Ward only, so those
payments cannot describe life Ward while the actor is choosing a target.

The optional `ward_life_card_traits` list supplies the printed fee, front or
back face, an already visible stable source reference, and current effective
status. It is omitted when empty. Identity discovery reads only projected
battlefield, graveyard, exile, stack, own or known cards, scoped search and
cost selections, and authenticated historical sources. It never enumerates
hidden cards. Exact duplicate references appear once in projection order.

Printed metadata comes from that visible definition. Effective status also
requires the same current battlefield incarnation, definition, owner and
controller. Trigger capture and observation share the printed Ward predicate:
executable definition, active printed abilities, front face for PayLife and
back face for BackFacePayLife. Historical incarnations cannot read a returned
object. Ability suppression retains the printed fee with effective=false.
The existing automatic back-face payment behavior is unchanged.

Two registered Standard/combined V8 fixture groups cover actor visibility,
revealed hand/library knowledge, hidden identity invariance, front/back face,
suppression, exact incarnation, deduplication and saved-state restoration.
The sixth actual Sire group additionally covers front-face fee visibility,
an actual Witness Protection cast, its life Ward payment and Aura removal.
These new groups are unrun. The earlier native receipt binds its earlier
source, not this extension.

Model admission still needs explicit FlatPolicyV5/native V5/Python features_v8
and Human V8 consumers. They must resolve transport references to authorized
visible rows, encode trait and payment relations with new feature identities,
preserve exact stack instances and payer affordability, and include optional
fields in canonical hashing. Frozen observations, tensor fields, contracts
and weights are unchanged. Existing scorers continue refusing schema8.
