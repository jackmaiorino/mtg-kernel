# Stack attributes: next representation contract constraints

This is preparation after the completed balanced-schedule replication, not a strength claim or a new training launch. Kimi's audit identifies public `kicked`, `is_flashback`, `cast_method`, `mode_chosen` and `x_value` as hash-only in the legacy model path. Its46/1,645 sampled decisions are reported exposure, not a causal explanation for the failed schedule or prevention experiments. Recompute exposure on the actual current decision path before choosing a training comparison.

The current `public_cost_features_v1.rs::from_actor_v4_v1` takes actor observation plus `object_card_ids`. Static cost rows are registry lookups by card token, and `validate_tokens` requires exactly those catalog rows. This is appropriate for printed cost, but cannot bind dynamic cast attributes to separate copies of the same card. Both native inference in `native_policy_value_net_v1/public_inputs_v1.rs` and training collection in `sideboard_play_policy_v1/public_inputs.rs` use this path. Do not append dynamic fields through a card-name/card-token join or change the existing V1 format in place.

A successor must preserve the tensorizer's explicit object/stack position mapping, or provide a separately ordered stack-item tensor with validated public edges. `native_flat_tensorizer_v2.rs::ObjectProjectionV2` already maps raw objects to tensor nodes; `FlatObjectCoreV2` retains group, source kind and visible ordinal. Audit how public stack entries map through these stages, including omitted/non-card stack items, before selecting the exact layout. No raw arena id, incarnation counter, hidden hand/library identity or private RNG value may become a feature. Unknown/absent fields must be distinct from valid zero values; categorical cast methods/modes need an explicit bounded vocabulary, not undocumented ordinal scaling.

Meaningful checks must include two same-card stack instances with different cast modes or X values, targets referring to the correct instance, multiple public stack orders, copied spells and non-card abilities, hidden-hand/library permutation, arena renumbering and replay. Verify the actual production inference tensors and archived training rows agree, not just a standalone feature helper. Zero-initialized projections must preserve logits, values, selected actions and optimizer continuation on matched replay. A deliberately nonzero projection must demonstrably affect the intended item and retain legal action mapping. Preserve completed source/checkpoint/analysis identities.

Keep stack information separate from static card-face expansion, curriculum, reward and optimizer changes for any later causal comparison. Costs/prevention can remain part of the versioned architecture, but do not silently enable them in the stack-only treatment. A bounded experiment still needs a specific question, matched controls, fresh seeds, frozen paired/breadth gates and qualified useful throughput. No broad campaign or paid compute follows from this note.
