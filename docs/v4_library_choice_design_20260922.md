# Proposed V4 library-choice sampler extension

2026-09-22. Draft for fresh design review after the outstanding sampler clarification. No source implementation or experiment launch. Motivation: the completed frozen E controller aborts9of16 consumed matches at library-choice roots. Correct coverage is the next prerequisite; neither those aborts nor the seven selected completions justify value/budget tuning or a strength claim.

Contract: preserve chooser-visible identities, selected prefix, legal card multiset, normalized action order, tensor/key/token and known positional facts, while resampling all otherwise unknown own-library positions. Do not pin matching cards in their hidden slots. Current whole-object movement preserves identity without relabeling. All-matching libraries are an explicit counterexample to candidate-slot pinning. Candidate counts and duplicates are information; unseen order is not. Scope is the three currently supported own-library forms SearchLibraryToHand, SearchLibraryToHandMany and SearchLibraryToBattlefieldTapped, including empty legal menus and partial multi-selection. Current engine validation already requires chooser==owner. Unsupported or malformed contexts remain typed errors, never ordinary-policy fallback.

Versioning: retain the exact existing clone/core/report/mean/E-v1 entry points and their library rejection. Add a LibraryChoiceV2 internal sampling mode with an explicit clone entry point, threaded through an additive core/bound-report call. Add an evaluation-only V4InformationSetEstimateV2 package variant with strict new schema/algorithm constants and a separately typed evaluation record; unchanged g115, E backup/ties, budgets and ordinary opponent. Old wrappers select legacy mode; non-library roots in V2 call the existing sampler without changing RNG draws or output. Training/collection/supported Disabled-only gates and old descriptor bytes remain unchanged. No activation or observer route is silently upgraded.

Clone transaction, proposed: validate the original pending choice and capture its exact visible/action boundary first. Build a typed plan for that specific choice identifying owner, form, filter, structural path, original bindings and selected/legal identities. On a disposable clone, permit only that validated search snapshot/candidate reference set to be rebuilt; retain every unrelated effect/stack/reference/residue conflict check. Shuffle all unknown own-library whole objects with the current canonical-pool convention and preserve existing known-position constraints. Keep normal opponent sampling. Rebuild original_library to the sampled order and regenerate raw legal candidates using existing engine validation/sort semantics, retaining selected identities and removing them from remaining choices. The engine's same-name raw tie-break uses library position, so copying an old raw legal vector is invalid. Revalidate the pending interpreter contract, rebuild session candidates/caches, then require full V4 boundary equality. Failure publishes nothing and never mutates the original session or retries a seed.

Implementation audit must identify every alias of the snapshot in pending choice, frames, answered guards and session origin decision. A narrow crate-private helper in effect.rs may reuse private candidate constructors/validators on a cloned state; it must not alter normal engine rules or silently normalize invalid inputs. Unrelated continuation references remain rejected. Do not simply erase pending state from the conflict scan, skip validation, pin the entire library, or redraw until a sample passes. Design review must resolve the exact safe exception and reconstruction before implementation.

Acceptance: both seats and all three forms; zero/one/all matching and duplicate cards; partial multi-selection; known-top preservation; same-seed byte equality and distinct-seed hidden diversity where possible; equivalent hidden permutations give the same canonical sampled world/key/tensor/menu; candidate/selected identities preserved; every available action binds the same physical card through a valid direct step; skip/final-selection and subsequent shuffle/draw are covered. Fault injection must reject changed card identity, stale generation, wrong owner/path/filter, altered selected prefix and unrelated bound-reference conflicts. Original state, policy tensors and policy RNG remain unchanged. Legacy rejection and old ordinary/E-v1 descriptor/result fixtures remain exact. Then use fixed previously aborted E trajectories for small actual-g115 correctness checks before any whole-match availability rerun. Do not count replayed identities as fresh strength samples.

Literature: collab/lit/20260922-v4-library-choice-boundary.md, POMCP observation conditioning and ISMCTS information-set distinction. Our sampler remains a declared convention, not a Bayesian posterior or convergence guarantee. Fixed-state acceptance has0trainingreplicas/no win-rate estimand; no identifiable effect against SD0.76pp/sensitivity1.39pp and no M1 success. Any subsequent consumed-frame availability run needs its own frozen design, current guarded serial/parallel throughput and full retained outcomes; formal strength requires powered n, joint gates and monitor/seed authority. Local small correctness builds only after design/source readiness, four BelowNormal build jobs on E, no GPU/paid compute. No engine-rule or whitelist changes are proposed. M1 remains unmet.
