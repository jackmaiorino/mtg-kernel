**Boundary.** Support the three existing own-library forms SearchLibraryToHand, SearchLibraryToHandMany and SearchLibraryToBattlefieldTapped, with chooser == owner == actor. Preserve physical identities/generations, selected prefix, legal multiset, normalized executable candidates, visible observation, tensor, action slice/key/token and known positions. Uniformly resample unknown whole objects using the existing canonical-pool convention. No slot pinning, retries, fallback or broad reference exemption.

**Correction 1, exact scope.** A crate-private effect plan first runs the original pending-choice validator and captures the complete choice contract, including form, owner, chooser, path, filter, selected bindings and legal multiset. Only pending.choice.original_library and its legal order may be rebuilt on a disposable clone. Selected bindings never change. No search frame exists at a valid choice root; every frame, answered guard, stack and unrelated residue/reference conflict check remains unchanged. The reference walker may exempt only the exact choice matching the validated plan. Rebuild uses bind_library_exact and library_search_candidates minus selected, checks physical legal multiset equality and reruns the existing validator.

**Correction 2, live origin.** Factor the engine's SelectTargets-to-Decision match arm into a crate-private pure helper, used by both normal engine and clone. Regenerate the session origin from the rebuilt pending choice before rebuilding raw candidates. Assert raw candidates follow the rebuilt legal order, then apply existing V3 normalization and require exact original candidate bytes and complete V4 boundary equality. The prior stored-origin proof alone was self-referential and is insufficient.

**Correction 3, retained RNG limitation.** Engine RNG state remains cloned, unchanged. A known-position card followed by a search shuffle is sent to the same future slot in every clone and the real game even after library knowledge clears. This is pre-existing future-randomness leakage, especially direct at library-search roots. A documenting counterexample is required. D2 does not change engine randomness or claim to resolve it. Any small guarded RNG isolation change needs a separately explicit scope disposition through the same engineering queue entry; D3's design verdict must explicitly address this limitation before an information-set strength claim. No Bayesian posterior or convergence claim.

**Correction 4, mandatory edge cases.** Empty Finish-only menus and partially completed many-selection are first-class acceptance fixtures, not optional. Cover both seats, all three forms, duplicate Forests, all-matching identical/distinct names, zero matches, selected prefix, scry-known top with duplicates. Prove same-seed equality, hidden diversity where possible, canonical equivalence under unknown permutation, all boundary representations/candidate bytes, unchanged original/policy RNG, physical target binding through direct step and subsequent shuffle/draw. Reject identity/generation/owner/path/filter/selected corruption, unrelated bound references and injected live search frames. Non-library V2 and legacy clones must be byte-identical; legacy library rejection remains.

**Versioning and scope.** Add LibraryChoiceV2 sampling mode and explicit clone/core/report routes, with existing callers delegating to legacy. Evaluation-only V4InformationSetEstimateV2 descriptor/record gets new strict schema/algorithm constants. Disabled, mean and E-v1 bytes and training/collection admission remain unchanged.
