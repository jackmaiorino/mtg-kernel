## What was built

- **Exporter** (`native_checkpoint_export_v1`, module, bin and tests): the historical native-inference-export/v1 producer from 18f4ca51, ported with its metadata schema, parameter encoding and checks unchanged. It loads a Store generation through the strict `load_checkpoint_v1` authorities, refuses weights-genesis and search-distillation inputs, and records its own production build (the build capture refuses dirty trees).
- **Round trip** (`native_inference_export_roundtrip_v1`): strict Store load, payload re-hash, import through `load_expanded_inference_v1`, and a bit-exact comparison of every named tensor across the import, bundle, payload section and Store model. Receipts are schema v1 for current-1 and v2 for the seven R14 members (v2 adds `import_route`).
- **R14 route** (`sideboard_play_policy_v1::registry_evolution_v1`): a versioned, inference-only import that admits exactly the committed allowlist of registry differences, keyed to the pinned (16d308da, ef738001) pair and the source card-DB hash a06fa9566106f0ea. Its loader is crate-private; learner initialization refuses R14 descriptors, and inference refuses them with a successor checkpoint. `load_v1`, `load_feature_transfer_v3`, `validate_card_namespace` and the ordinary learner loader are unchanged.
- **Registry snapshots**: `data/line_a_source_registries/` holds af9d7256 (18f4ca51 era) and 16d308da (1804e9f9 era), plus the allowlist (2f883b5e).
- **Tools**: the manifest generator, the allowlist writer, the semantics report and the admission receipt (collector interface, revision 3 in the shape the collector parses: an R14 receipt names the Fable section, Codex's countersign note and the accepted commit, each verified to exist before writing, and the smoke evaluator must be built at that commit; registry_pins carries the loaded registry and namespace identity for the collector to compare), all in `python/tools/`.
- **Sealed evidence** under `E:/mtg-line-a-panel-imports-20260927` (catalogued): bundles, descriptors, round-trip and smoke receipts, card-DB hash recomputation, lineage parents, registry linkage, the semantics report (96c027b6) and admission receipts. Binaries are pinned under `E:/pinned-binaries/<sha256>/`.

## HaleysPC check-only receipt binding (CODEX #627)

Collection now compares the completion's plan digest with the exact local `plan.json` bytes, checks that local state agrees with that plan, and requires a completed Cargo command to equal the command derived from it. A fetched `cargo.log` must match the producer's SHA-256, which the runner also records for partial logs when a job errors or is refused. The receipt includes the plan and log digests.

Nonzero Cargo exits remain failed test/build evidence; early refusals and errors without a Cargo log remain receipted. Missing or mismatched binding evidence is refused. Older error records with a partial log but no producer digest cannot establish log equality and are refused by the repaired collector.

Validation: 17 focused offline tests across `ReceiptTests`, `ControllerTests` and `DispatchTests` in `python/tests/test_haley_check_only_v1.py`. No remote call, install, build, GPU work or measurement was made for this repair. Independent transport-use review and remote qualification remain pending.
