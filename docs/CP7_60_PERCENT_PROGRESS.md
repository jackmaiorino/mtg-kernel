## Why the plan changed

The generation-896 native D_out initial measurement finished cleanly, but its overall classification was `FAIL`.

- Generation 896 beat the imported parent by 87 wins in 4,096 games and passed that comparison.
- Generation 896 lost to terminal generation 2048 by 5 wins, 2,299 to 2,304, and failed every predeclared gate for that comparison.
- Generation 896's measured rate in that native panel was 56.13%, but this was not a CP7 panel.
- Because the required terminal-control comparison failed, confirmation and CP7 were correctly blocked.

The next useful question is therefore not "Can we rerun generation 896?" It is "Can an independent search teacher reliably choose actions that lead to better natural game outcomes, and can a learner absorb that signal?"

The strong-neural-pressure A/B also finished cleanly and failed. PRESSURE did not reliably beat CONTROL and did not beat frozen g896. Its descriptive win rate against promoted(2) was 54.09%, between CONTROL at 53.71% and g896 at 54.37%. This closes the tested opponent-quality lever and leaves CP7 untouched.

The sustained STRUCTURED continuation also finished cleanly and failed. STRUCTURED was descriptively better than its matched CONTROL, 54.35% versus 53.69%, but the paired confidence lower bound remained negative. Against frozen g896 it was effectively tied, 54.35% versus 54.32%, with only 2 paired points of separation. This closes the exact 256-update structured residual route and leaves CP7 untouched.

A fixed-batch diagnostic then measured what happened inside that learner. By update 256, its policy was still nearly identical to g896: mean action-distribution movement was 0.20%, and its greedy action changed on only 0.41% of contestable decisions. The trust bound was not active. On the shared model, the weighted value gradient was 18.7 times the policy gradient and pointed slightly against it. This supports one narrow follow-up: repeat from exact g896 with the same terminal win/loss policy objective and opponent schedule, but freeze the value path so only the policy signal can update shared features.

That policy-only follow-up also finished cleanly and failed. It was descriptively almost identical to both comparators: 54.71% versus promoted(2), compared with 54.59% for joint STRUCTURED and 54.68% for frozen g896. Removing value-loss interference did not create a material native strength gain. This closes the exact policy-only objective, architecture, trust bound, 256-update budget, and opponent schedule.
