## Research consequence

The combined controls now demonstrate three distinct requirements: avoid self-lethal, take face lethal when removal loses, and remove a creature when face damage fails. The failed representation/entropy screens remain failed. A narrow terminal-outcome teacher has an advantage on these exact public positions, but this diagnostic still clones a synthetic engine state and does not establish a deployable fair-information teacher on arbitrary live positions. The completed natural census found only one missed immediate win, which the model later converted to a win anyway. Natural frequency and whole-match value of this stronger mistake remain unknown.

Next build the data and information boundary for a bounded teacher-learning feasibility check, before another training campaign: accept only actor-visible inputs, require complete natural terminal labels for each compared action, abstain at hidden or discretionary continuations, and include both face-required and creature-required families. Keep these already inspected fixtures as development controls; predeclare structurally different evaluation positions before scoring a learned descendant. Compare unchanged g115, a terminal-label learner and a matched label-permuted control. A fitting success on these few fixtures would not establish generalization or authorize promotion.
