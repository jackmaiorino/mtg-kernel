**Boarding philosophy by deck (one line each)**

- **Wildfire:** Swaps slow value (Nihil Spellbomb, Blood Fountain, Lembas) for hand disruption (Duress) and precise, matchup-specific creature/artifact answers.
- **Rally:** Cuts card selection (Reckless Impulse, Experimental Synthesizer) for sweepers and reach, since its own board and most foes run one-toughness creatures.
- **Affinity:** Trims slow equipment and singleton value for mana denial (Gorilla Shaman), color hate (Pyroblast/Hydroblast), and small sweepers matched to the opposing creature base.
- **Elves:** Replaces fragile utility (Wellwisher, Quirion Ranger) with bigger reach bodies (Vitu-Ghazi Inspector) and board-scaling removal (Monstrous Emergence).
- **Spy:** Cuts ramp and card selection for graveyard disruption and cheap removal (Fume Spitter, Masked Vandal, Mesmeric Fiend) to blunt combo assembly.
- **Burn:** Benches the uncastable off-color card (Sneaky Snacker) for more burn or color hosers tuned to each opponent's palette.
- **Terror:** Swaps passive card draw for color hosers (Hydroblast, Pyroblast, Annul) and cheap removal (Gut Shot) against creature-heavy fields.
- **CawGates:** Drops taxing or undersized cards for artifact hate (Dust to Dust) and countermagic/color hate protecting its own plan.
- **Faeries:** Trades card draw for hard answers (Annul, Steel Sabotage) and a cheap lock piece (Bind the Monster), since its board rarely wins combat alone.
