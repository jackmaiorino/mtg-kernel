FACT-CHECK REPORT: PAUPER SIDEBOARD PLAN TABLE

Scope: 81 plans, 1188 claims checked. 10 plans found unsound and repaired. 0 plans still illegal after repair.

REPAIRED PLANS (10)

1. Wildfire>Elves: The premise "Elves never uses its graveyard" was false. Masked Vandal exiles a creature from its own graveyard as an ETB cost, and sideboard Pulse of Murasa recurs creatures Wildfire kills. Four other cited cards (Eviscerator's Insight, Blood Fountain, Nyxborn Hydra, Lembas) are not graveyard hate at all.

2. Wildfire>CawGates: Basilisk Gate and Squadron Hawk have no lock ability of any kind. CawGates does use its own graveyard (Sacred Cat's Embalm, Prismatic Strands' Flashback), so Nihil Spellbomb has real disruptive value there.

3. Wildfire>Faeries: Breath Weapon is a symmetric sweep hitting all non-Dragons on both sides, not an opponent-only flier sweep, and would kill three of Wildfire's own creatures. No Faeries flier actually has 2 toughness. Faeries does discard into its graveyard (Moon-Circuit Hacker, Harrier Strix), just without a payoff.

4. Affinity>Spy: Spy's registered list does not run Undercity Informer; only Balustrade Spy self-mills. Troll of Khazad-dum is black, not green.

5. Elves>Spy: Spy is not removal-free (Fume Spitter) and not without a damage plan (Lotleth Giant pings on Undergrowth).

6. Elves>Faeries: Reach is not unique to Vitu-Ghazi Inspector; Generous Ent and the surviving Nyxborn Hydra also have it.

7. Burn>Burn (mirror): Sneaky Snacker has a graveyard-return trigger on a player's third card drawn that turn, supported by the mirror's own loot effects, so it is not "guaranteed dead" for either side.

8. CawGates>Spy: Lead the Stampede and Land Grant do not mill and do not feed Lotleth Giant's graveyard count.

9. Faeries>Elves: Nyxborn Hydra is an enchantment creature and a legal Annul target. Elves' sideboard runs Pulse of Murasa, an instant, so Dispel is not fully dead post-board.

10. Faeries>Spy: Spy's sideboard contains a red instant (Flaring Pain), so "no red cards" and "no instants" were both false.

All ten corrections have been applied to the rationale text; the repaired entries are ready to ratify as written.
