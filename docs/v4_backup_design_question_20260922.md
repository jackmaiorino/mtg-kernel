Proposed statistic, distinct from unchanged empirical mean sums: each node has a root-perspective H. At an unexpanded leaf H is its original neural evaluation. At an expanded node H is max(root actor) or min(opponent actor) of estimates for visited edges. Unvisited edges are omitted, explicitly creating small-sample optimism/pessimism rather than a bound. For an edge, retain counts for natural terminal outcomes, direct cutoff evaluations and each linked live child. E=(terminal sum+direct cutoff sum+sum(child traversal count*child H))/edge visits. A newly linked child uses its neural H until expanded. Root coverage and depth/budget cutoffs contribute actual direct samples; never silently drop early negative samples or rewrite empirical mean statistics. Natural terminal sample contributes once per actual occurrence, no sticky proof flag.
