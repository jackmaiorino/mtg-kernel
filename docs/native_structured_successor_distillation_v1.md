# Native structured successor distillation v1

## Question

Can the complete-public-history structured model first reproduce the retained
parent as a standalone policy and value function, so a later terminal-only
on-policy rung does not need sparse outcomes to learn both Rally semantics and
policy improvement at once?

This is an initialization screen. Parent outputs are dense supervision only.
They are not rewards, promotion evidence, or a substitute for a fresh win gate.
Natural terminal win, draw, or loss remains the only reward and the only
promotion measure in the subsequent reinforcement-learning phase.

## Fixed screen

- Source cache SHA-256:
  `280e34cd7f685beaf52c1cab3b41c53613a5029c063871942f48c063b6f5996f`.
- Source population: 2,048 seat-swapped Rally pairs generated by the retained
  parent against the frozen Pool3 `40/20/20/20` mixture.
- Inputs: complete public history, structured state, objects, relations,
  actions, references, and the existing digest features.
- Architecture: width 48 and the last 16 complete public physical decisions.
- Output mode: standalone absolute policy logits and value. The retained
  parent is not added to the student output.
- Targets: the exact retained-parent action distribution and value already
  committed in each candidate-policy row.
- Split: four folds by whole pair, with `pair_index mod 4` held out.
- Objective: teacher-to-student policy KL plus value MSE. Every episode has
  equal total mass, every physical decision has equal mass within its episode,
  and autoregressive substeps share their physical decision's mass equally.
- Fit: five epochs, batch size 64 physical decisions, AdamW learning rate
  `3e-4`, weight decay `1e-4`, gradient norm cap 5, seed `20260803`.

A 128-pair, one-epoch run may select thread topology and estimate wall time.
It cannot change these scientific settings and is not efficacy evidence.

## Frozen parity gates

Aggregate the four held-out folds. Pass only if every condition holds:

1. Weighted mean policy total variation is at most `0.020`.
2. Weighted p90 policy total variation is at most `0.050`.
3. Mean total variation is at most `0.025` for each candidate seat.
4. Top-action agreement is at least `0.980` overall and `0.970` for each seat.
5. Value RMSE is at most `0.10` overall and `0.12` for each seat.
6. Every individual fold has mean total variation at most `0.025`.

These thresholds define practical parent parity, not exact logit equality. A
failure closes this width-48 distillation attempt without tuning against the
revealed folds.

## Pass disposition

A pass authorizes one full-data fit with the identical settings, strict
Python/Rust inference parity, and a short native matched noninferiority gate
against the retained parent. Only after that gate passes will one terminal-only
on-policy Pool3 rung be frozen and run from the distilled successor. Search,
opponent-conditioned heads, residual stacks, and shaped rewards remain out of
scope.

The external XMage baseline remains descriptive: promoted(2) scored `46-66`
across 112 CP7 games, with the frozen 64-game block at `26-38`. No result in
this branch establishes CP7 superiority, cross-deck strength, human strength,
or pro-level play.
